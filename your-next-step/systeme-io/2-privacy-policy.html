Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Living Lyfe on Purpose LLC (“Living Lyfe on Purpose,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides coaching with Nechelle Marie. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, book a coaching session, or contact us, and how we use and protect it.

Information we collect

Information you give us:

  • Your name, email address, and any other details you enter when you book a session, such as your answers to booking questions.
  • Messages you send us by email.
  • What you choose to share with us during coaching sessions.

Payment information: payments are processed by Stripe or PayPal. We do not see or store your full card or bank details.

Technical information: our website, scheduling, and payment providers may automatically collect basic technical details, such as your IP address, browser type, and device, so their services work properly. We do not use advertising pixels or analytics tracking on our website.

How we use your information

  • To schedule and deliver your coaching session, including sending confirmations, reminders, and Zoom details.
  • To process your payment.
  • To respond to your questions.
  • To meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not add you to our email list when you book. You join our email list only if you ask to.

Confidentiality of your sessions

What you share in a coaching session stays between us. We will not share it with anyone without your permission, unless the law requires us to or we believe there is a serious risk of harm to you or someone else.

Any notes from your session are kept private and used only to support your coaching. You can request a copy of your session notes at any time by emailing us.

Services we use

We rely on trusted providers to run our business. Each one handles your information under its own privacy policy:

  • Calendly, for scheduling.
  • Zoom, for coaching sessions.
  • Stripe and PayPal, for payments.
  • systeme.io, for our website and email.

Cookies

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Our website, scheduling, and payment providers may use cookies that are necessary for their services to work. You can manage or block cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work without them.

How long we keep your information

We keep your information only as long as we need it to provide our services and meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations. After that, we delete it or remove anything that identifies you.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete your information, unless we must keep it by law.
  • Send you a copy of your information.
  • Stop using your information for a particular purpose.

To make a request, email nechelle@iamnechellemarie.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. Depending on where you live, including the European Union and the United Kingdom, you may have additional rights, including the right to file a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Clients outside the United States

We are based in the United States, and our service providers may store and process your information in the United States. By booking a session from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No website, email, or online service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Age requirement

Our services are for adults 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email nechelle@iamnechellemarie.com.
