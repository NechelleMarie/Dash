Introducing Your Next Step

One private hour that’s about you.

Your Next Step is a 60-minute, one-on-one coaching session on Zoom. It’s focused time to sort through what’s on your mind and decide what comes next.

It’s for you if you’re the strong one, the dependable one, the one everyone leans on, and you’re ready to give your own goals some attention. You might feel overwhelmed. You might be weighing a decision. You might be ready for a change and unsure where to start. Each of those is a good reason to book.

Here’s how we’ll spend our time

  1. Talk it through. You share the situation that’s weighing on you, in your own words and at your own pace.
  2. Find what needs you first. We sort out what’s pressing, what matters most, and what can wait.
  3. Choose one priority. One clear focus, so your time and energy have somewhere to go.
  4. Explore a boundary. We look at one practical boundary that protects the room that priority needs.
  5. Build your seven-day plan. Realistic steps you can take this week, shaped around your real schedule.

One session won’t settle every question in your life. It’s designed to give you a clear place to start and a plan you can act on this week.

Questions

Before you book

Who is this session for?

Women who are used to being the strong one. If people come to you for advice and support while your own goals keep getting pushed aside, this session is for you. It’s a good fit whether you’re feeling overwhelmed, facing a decision, or ready for a change. Women of all beliefs are welcome.

What if I don’t know where to start?

That’s completely okay, and it’s more common than you think. You don’t need a clear question or a plan before you book. Come as you are. We’ll talk it through together and find your starting point.

What will we work on?

We’ll talk through the situation weighing on you, identify what needs your attention first, choose one clear priority, explore a practical boundary that supports it, and create a realistic action plan for the next seven days.

Is this therapy?

No. Your Next Step is coaching. It is not therapy or mental health treatment. Our work focuses on priorities, decisions, boundaries, and practical next steps. If you’re looking for mental health support, please reach out to a licensed mental health professional.
