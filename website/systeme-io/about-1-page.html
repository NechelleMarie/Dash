About

I Know This Question From the Inside

Empowerment Strategist · Certified Life Coach · Founder of People of Valor

I am Nechelle Marie. I have overcome everything that was meant to break me, and I rebuilt my life on a foundation of faith, truth, and people who would not let me stay stuck.

I know what it’s like to be the one everyone counts on. I was the loyal employee who never called off, the resourceful friend to everyone, and the cheerleader for people who only came around when they needed help.

When I let go of those roles, I felt like a bird that had been set free. What I didn’t know at the time was that my peace was being restored.

Today, I walk alongside women standing where I once stood, and I give them what I needed most: a path. As the founder of the People of Valor community, I help women make room for their own needs and move forward with clarity.
